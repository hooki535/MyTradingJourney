A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clearer start.

This is your space to learn, practice, and reflect at your own pace.

What is MyTradingJourney?+

A personal trading and growth workspace. It connects chart replay, trade journaling, reading, mindset, business experiments, and weekly reflection.

Where should I begin?+

Choose one small action: study a setup in Backtesting Lab, open a Book Hub, or record a decision in your Trade Journal. The quick guide links you to each space.

Is this a trading signal or mentorship service?+

No. MyTradingJourney is a personal learning and practice project. The tools help organize your own research and reflections; they don’t provide signals or promise returns.

How does the Learning Hub work?+

Each of the 24 books opens a guide with big ideas, original application frameworks, a seven-day plan, journal prompts, and a daily checklist. You can add a PDF and keep learning notes.

Where are my notes and PDFs saved?+

Existing workspace records and account PDFs use your signed-in account. New Book Hub progress saves on the current device; previously saved device PDFs stay on that device. Hub JSON export/import lets you move progress, while PDF files need to be kept separately.

Will the new design change my existing workspace?+

Your existing workspace, accounts, book covers, and PDF tools remain available. Use “Access my workspace” to return to your tools.
